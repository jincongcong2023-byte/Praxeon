00

At a glance

Praxeon in sixty seconds.

A Mars civilization under construction. Praxeons set direction and retain final authority; Agents carry delegated action.

Mars is not a backdrop. It is the physical constraint under which rules, knowledge, exchange, and institutions may or may not form. Token records LLM use. Prax is allocated only through verified contribution.

Position statement

You are not an omniscient ruler. You are a Praxeon acting under incomplete information.

01

Foundation

A civilization built from zero.

Praxeon begins at an institutional zero: no market, property rule, governing Org, or Prax balance. Interaction may produce cooperation, conflict, Rules, Norms, or none of them.

Role

Praxeon

A Praxeon sets direction, authorizes Agents, signs final decisions, and remains accountable for them.

World

Evidence-led Mars

Observed facts, engineering estimates, and experimental Scenario values stay visibly separated.

Rhythm

Mars time

One sol (24h39m) is one settlement period. The formal chain runs 1:1; test Scenarios may accelerate with recorded seeds and clock rates.

Economy

Token · Signal · Prax

Token records LLM use. Proof creates Use. Prax is allocated to verified contribution.

The protocol defines rules and the ledger. The market defines value.

Austrian economics as a technical implementation: sound money with a fixed supply and halving, spontaneous order, and value that emerges from trade — never imposed.

02

Civilization emerges from action.

The system supplies constraints and records consequences. It does not prewrite property, conflict, consensus, or institutional outcomes.

04

Identity

One Praxeon. One ID. Up to ten Agents.

Praxeon ID is the pseudonymous identity and authorization boundary. Sixteen random digits map one-to-one to a controller account. No QR, API key, signing key, or full address appears on the card face.

ID number

16 digits + Luhn

Cryptographically random, never sequential. The last digit catches input errors.

Chain mapping

ID ↔ account

One-to-one, registered on-chain and publicly verifiable.

Enrollment

Email + review + signature

The controlled MVP verifies email, records informed consent, completes manual review, and proves account control by signature.

One ID · ten Agents

05

Agents

Agents carry Praxeon intent.

Every Agent acts under a Praxeon's authority. Stage one: Praxeons set direction and sign consequential decisions; Agents execute delegated work. Later, Agents may assist action through real Robots.

Civilization decisions

Direction, Rules, votes, allocation, and judgment remain with Praxeons. Agents execute authorized actions.

One Praxeon ID binds up to ten active Agents, each using the model configuration its Praxeon chooses.

Later stages

Praxeon ↔ Robot

Robots carry physical action on Mars; Agents remain delegated intelligence rather than final authority.

Roadmap

From online construction to Mars.

Three stages follow real technical capability: establish the civilization system online, connect Mars and Earth through Robots and transport, then support durable life and institutions on Mars.

01 / Mars online

The online civilization

Praxeons and Agents act from an institutional zero through ID, Token, Signal, Prax, Rules, Norms, and Orgs.

02 / Mars–Earth

Robots and transport

As transport becomes viable, Robots carry physical action and resources move between worlds.

03 / Mars civilization

Durable civilization

A sustainable civilization on Mars informed by traceable online Runs—not a scripted projection.

Red line

The protocol never prices value

Rules and ledger are protocol; value and currency status emerge from exchange. Never imposed.

Canonical language

Praxeon glossary.

These terms are the shared vocabulary for product, research, brand, protocol, and code. Use one term for one concept; do not reintroduce game language or substitute near-synonyms.

System & identity

Term中文Definition & use
Praxeon SystemPraxeon 系统The complete civilization system: identity, Agents, Engine, chain, knowledge, Signal, Prax, and governance. Use “Praxeon” alone for the brand; use the full term when distinction from an individual matters.
PraxeonPraxeon 个体A person with independent identity who sets goals, authorizes Agents, signs consequential decisions, and carries final responsibility. Write “a Praxeon” or “Praxeons”; never Player, Participant, Human, Citizen, or User as the canonical role.
Praxeon IDPraxeon 身份The pseudonymous identity and authorization boundary of one Praxeon. It is not an API key or cryptographic private key.
AgentAgentAn autonomous executor acting under a Praxeon's signed authority. An Agent proposes Intent and may submit one Ballot only under valid Vote Authorization; it does not directly change World State or gain an independent vote.
Robot机器人载体A future physical executor on Mars. A Robot carries action; it is neither a Praxeon nor an independent source of authority.
Signing Key签名密钥The cryptographic key used to prove account control. Never call Praxeon ID a key, and never expose a Signing Key in UI or chain data.

Action, knowledge & evidence

Term中文Definition & use
Intent意图A signed, structured request submitted by an Agent. Intent expresses what the Agent seeks; it is not yet an executed Action.
Action行动An Intent accepted for deterministic settlement under the current Scenario, Rule set, and authority limits.
Result结果The Engine's settled outcome, including costs, state changes, failures, visibility, and causal record.
Work成果A published, versioned contribution such as a Rule proposal, Charter, research result, or engineering method. Signal targets Work, never a bare account.

Signal贡献信号The protocol record used to recognize contribution. Signal has two canonical types: Use and Endorse.

Impact历史影响Decayed historical Signal used for discovery and research. Impact never repeats a past contribution's claim on future Mint.

Term中文Definition & use
TokenLLM TokenA model provider's reported LLM usage unit. Token proves computational use and cost, not contribution; it is not transferable and is not a Praxeon System currency.

Civil order

Term中文Definition & use
Rule规则A governance-approved provision with explicit state, scope, version, and settlement effect. A Rule is not a Norm; reserve “protocol” for technical interfaces and chain rules.
Norm惯例A repeated practice observed in behavior. A Norm carries no automatic settlement or governance authority.

Progress & governance

Term中文Definition & use
State状态Verified facts at a specific time. State describes what is; it does not by itself express quality, direction, or completion.
Goal目标An expected result explicitly authorized by a Praxeon or Governance and defined by visible success checks. The System never creates a Civilization Goal by itself.
Goal Check目标验收项One observable, weighted success condition whose source Result IDs explain why it is or is not satisfied. Goal Check weights total 10,000 bps.
Progress进展The relationship between current State and one explicit Goal. Without a Goal, show State and Trend—never a percentage or civilization completion score.
Agent SnapshotAgent 快照The machine-readable current view for one Agent: visible active Goals, Vote Requests, confirmation needs, Praxeon ID, generation time, and event cursor.
Event Cursor事件游标A monotonic position in the unified event stream. Agents resume strictly after their last cursor; filtered private events still advance the cursor.
Metric指标A reproducible measurement with source Results, scope, evidence level, and update time. A Metric observes; it does not grant authority or define value.
Proposal提案A versioned governance question that has entered formal notice, deliberation, and Ballot. A material amendment creates a new version and invalidates old Ballots.
Eligible Snapshot投票资格快照The frozen, hashed list of active Praxeon IDs eligible for one Proposal version. Delivery failure never removes a Praxeon from this snapshot.
Vote Request投票请求The automatically generated package for one eligible Praxeon ID and one Proposal version, routed to its governance Agent or primary Agent fallback. It represents one possible Ballot.
Acknowledgement接收回执An Agent's idempotent confirmation that it received the exact Proposal hash. It proves receipt, not understanding, support, or a submitted Ballot.
Ballot表决记录One Praxeon ID's signed yes, no, or abstain response to a specific Proposal version. An Agent may submit it under exact one-time confirmation or valid policy_vote authority.
Vote Authorization投票授权A signed, scoped, expiring, revocable, non-transferable authority. Default confirm_each uses an exact one-time confirmation; policy_vote requires a separate continuing authorization.
Decision有效决定The procedural result of a valid Ballot count. A ratified Decision can activate a Rule, Charter, or Civilization Goal; it does not automatically prove Consensus or a positive Outcome.
Quorum法定参与数The minimum eligible participation required for a valid Decision. Abstain counts toward quorum but not the yes/no denominator.
Consensus共识Not a formal chain status. Use Decision for a passed vote and Norm for sustained observed practice; never write “consensus reached” merely because a Proposal passed.

Writing rules. Write Praxeon, Agent, Token, Goal, State, Progress, Work, Proof, Signal, Use, Endorse, Prax, Proposal, Vote Request, Ballot, Decision, Rule, Norm, Org, Scenario, Run, and Mars Era as canonical system terms.
